what actually happens when you visit, in full

Simple News doesn't have accounts, cookies, analytics, or trackers of any kind that we add ourselves. Here is a complete, specific account of what actually touches your browser or your data, so this holds up to inspection rather than just being a slogan.

What we don't do

  • No account, sign-up, or login.
  • No cookies set by this site.
  • No analytics, ad trackers, or third-party scripts of any kind.
  • No fonts, images, or scripts loaded from third-party CDNs, everything the page needs (fonts included) is hosted on this same site.

What runs only in your browser, and stays there

Two small preferences are saved with the browser's built-in localStorage, which never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else:

  • Dark/light mode - which theme you last picked.
  • Read/unread state - which headlines you've clicked, so they dim on your next visit.

You can clear both at any time through your browser's site data settings, and clearing them has no effect on anything server-side because there is no server-side record of them to begin with.

What's outside our control

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub's infrastructure necessarily sees the request that fetches this page (your IP address, browser, and the URL requested) in order to serve it, the same as any website you visit. That's GitHub's server operation, governed by GitHub's own privacy statement, not something we configure, add to, or receive a copy of.

When you click a headline, you leave this site and land on the original source (Reddit, Hacker News, GitHub, Hugging Face, and so on), each governed by its own privacy policy from that point on.

How the site itself is built

The three news lists are generated on a schedule by an automated script that reads public APIs and feeds, no user data is involved in that process at all, it runs independently of anyone visiting the site. The truthiness labels are generated the same way, by a small language model running as part of that same automated build, described in full on the methodology page.
