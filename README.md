# Simple News

[![Update news feed](https://github.com/twinkites/simple-llm-news/actions/workflows/update-news.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/twinkites/simple-llm-news/actions/workflows/update-news.yml)

Local open-weight model news, in three short lists: **model news**,
**security news**, and **harness news** (llama.cpp, ollama, vLLM, and the
like). Pulled from r/LocalLLaMA, Hacker News, and official project release
feeds, refreshed a few times a day.

No tracking. Trying to provide a simplier platform for updates in the chaos. 



By TwinKites LLC.



## The colored dot 

That's the **truthiness label**. It's generated by a small open-weight
language model running as part of the site's own build process,
fittingly, since this whole site is about local open-weight models.

- **Green**: reads as sober and well-corroborated
- **Yellow**: mixed signals, worth a second look
- **Red**: hype-framed, unsourced, or barely corroborated

It is **not a fact-check**, and deliberately not a 0-100 score either. 

You probably should take it like your intake of salt - cautiously.

Small local models can't reliably do multi-step reasoning like "weigh the
source, the framing, and the vote count, then pick a number", they tend
to fabricate a plausible-sounding answer instead of actually doing the
comparison. So the two judgments are split by what each side can do
reliably: how many people upvoted it (and whether the source even has a
vote count, official release feeds don't) is a number the site already
has, so that part is a plain, deterministic calculation, no model
involved. The *only* thing asked of the model is whether the headline's
phrasing itself reads as sober or as hype/clickbait, a short text
judgment, not arithmetic. The two are then combined by a fixed rule, not
by the model. Think of it as a second opinion on framing, not a verdict
on truth.

Hover (or focus, on a keyboard) over the dot for the model's one-line
reason. For the exact model and the exact prompt it's given, see
[methodology.html](methodology.html), it's written out in full.
