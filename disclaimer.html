What this site does

Simple News republishes headlines and links, nothing more. For every item shown, we display a title, a link to the original source, and a small amount of metadata (where it came from, a vote count, when it was posted). We do not copy, host, or claim ownership of the underlying articles, posts, or their content. Clicking a headline takes you to the original publisher's site.

We don't vouch for linked content

Sources include Reddit (r/LocalLLaMA), Hacker News, and official project release feeds (GitHub, Hugging Face). We don't fact-check, edit, or endorse the content those links point to, and we aren't responsible for its accuracy, completeness, or opinions expressed there. Ranking on this site (by recency and community score) is not an endorsement.

The truthiness label is not a claim about anyone

Each headline is also shown with a colored label generated by a small automated language model, described in full on the methodology page. That label reflects the model's read of a headline's phrasing and how much social corroboration it has, it is not a statement by Twin Kites LLC that any linked source, author, or publisher is inaccurate, untrustworthy, or acting in bad faith. It's a rough signal, not a verdict, and it can be wrong.
